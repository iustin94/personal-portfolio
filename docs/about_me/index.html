About

My job has always been to understand the problem, then design and build the solution.

I am a business analyst and software architect based on Fyn, Denmark. For more than eleven years I have worked where a business question meets a technical one: whether a messaging design would survive Black Friday, how a ten-year-old risk platform could be replaced, why a volunteer association was losing money.

The method is the same each time:

  • Measure first. I baseline and test before I recommend anything.
  • Design simply. The model uses the words the business uses.
  • Build it. I write the software myself, mostly in C# and .NET.
  • Document clearly. Decisions and their reasons are written down, including what did not work.

I hold an M.Sc. in Computational Biomedicine and a B.Sc. in Software Development, and I am technical co-founder of Esio ApS. See what I offer and the case studies behind it.
