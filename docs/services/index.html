Services

Four ways to work with me, from understanding the problem to running the software. Take one, or the whole path.

Business analysis

Something costs too much time or money, and nobody has written down how it works today.

Your process, numbers and constraints mapped, and requirements a developer can build from.

What you get

  • A map of the current process, with the numbers that show where it loses time or money
  • The constraints stated plainly: budget, people, rules and deadlines
  • Options with their trade-offs, and one recommendation
  • Requirements written so a developer can build from them

Where I have done it

  • O’Town Makerspace — Organizational & Technical Leadership, O’Town Makerspace

Feasibility study

You are about to commit to a technology or a design, and nobody has measured whether it will hold.

A measured answer to whether a technology or a design will hold, before you commit money to it.

What you get

  • A baseline and a test plan agreed before testing starts
  • Reproducible tests against your own workload
  • A written report: what holds, what breaks and at what load
  • A recommendation: go, change the design, or stop

Where I have done it

  • Feasibility Study of RabbitMQ, Novicell
  • Evaluation of Rule‑Based Simulation Modeling, University of Southern Denmark (SDU)
  • MasSpec Data Analysis Pipeline, University of Southern Denmark (SDU)

Architecture & modernisation

Your system has become slow to change, or you are starting a new one and want it to stay changeable.

A design for the new system, or the old one moved to a clean domain model in steps that each ship.

What you get

  • A domain model in the words your business uses
  • A target architecture, with each decision and its reason written down
  • Prototypes for the risky parts
  • A migration path in steps that each ship

Where I have done it

  • Enterprise Process Modeling Platform, Ramboll

Product build

You have a defined need and want working software, not a document.

The software designed, built and running in production, by the person who analysed the problem.

What you get

  • Full-stack implementation, mostly in C# and .NET
  • Hosting, deployment and monitoring set up
  • Documentation another developer can take over from
  • Regular working versions you can try, not one delivery at the end

Where I have done it

  • GitDiffGraph: Reviewing a Code Change as a Graph, Independent
  • SaaS Platform for Financial Workflows, Independent / Entrepreneurial
  • RabbitMQ Monitoring Framework, Novicell
  • Drone Flight Assistant App, QuadSat
  • Experiment Orchestration App, Cognition and Behavior Laboratory
