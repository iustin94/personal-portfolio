O’Town Makerspace — Organizational & Technical Leadership

O’Town Makerspace · Odense, Denmark

  • Community Development
  • Organizational Leadership
  • Infrastructure

Problem
The makerspace ran at a continuous loss and risked using up its reserves.
Analysis
Mapped the real state of the finances, the membership billing and the recurring costs.
Solution
Simpler routines, corrected billing and fewer recurring costs. The association became financially positive for the first time since COVID.

Over several years I have become progressively more involved in O’Town Makerspace, a volunteer‑run creative community in Odense. What started as a personal interest in hands‑on engineering gradually evolved into a long-term commitment to strengthening the organization — operationally, financially, and technically. My focus has consistently been on understanding real constraints and delivering sustainable improvements together with other volunteers.

Roles

  • Nov 2023: Board member
  • 2024: Treasurer
  • 2025 – Present: Chairman

As Chairman

  • Organisational leadership, finances, events, recruitment, and volunteer coordination.
  • Operation and maintenance of advanced machinery: laser cutter, CNC machines, 3D printers, woodworking and metal-working equipment.
  • Operator and safety training for members on all machine types; development of procedures and work standards.
  • Troubleshooting and equipment maintenance; management of spare parts and supplier relationships.
  • Process optimisation of production workflows and machine utilisation to maximise member satisfaction and safety.

Financial Stabilization

When I stepped into the treasurer role, the makerspace was operating at a continuous loss and risked depleting its reserves. The situation required a structural, not superficial, response.

I approached it similarly to resolving technical debt:

  • mapping the current state honestly
  • simplifying outdated workflows
  • implementing predictable, transparent processes
  • fixing membership billing and accounting routines
  • removing unnecessary recurring costs

Outcome: the makerspace became financially positive for the first time since the COVID period, creating room for reinvestment and long‑term planning.

Technical Infrastructure & Digital Modernization

With a background in systems architecture, I led multiple efforts to modernize our digital operations:

  • complete redesign of the public website
  • secure hosting and backend modernization
  • implementation of access control and updated IT infrastructure
  • automations for routine administration
  • improved communication channels for members and the board

The goal was reliability and maintainability — systems that volunteers could operate without depending on a single person.

Improving the Physical Workspace

Alongside technical and administrative work, I collaborated with other members to enhance the practical environment:

  • reorganizing equipment for better workflow and safety
  • creating clearer zones for electronics, fabrication, and woodworking
  • improving accessibility and reducing friction for new members

These changes helped both onboarding and day‑to‑day usability, making the space feel more inviting.

Strategic Communication & Community Growth

I took responsibility for strengthening our public presence:

  • managing social media
  • coordinating marketing and outreach
  • clarifying our messaging and identity
  • supporting member recruitment

This contributed to increased awareness and a steady rise in member interest.

Leadership Approach

Although I have initiated and led many projects, all outcomes have been the result of collaborative work with board members and volunteers. My focus has been to:

  • set a clear direction
  • break large initiatives into achievable steps
  • coordinate volunteers effectively
  • maintain transparency in decision-making
  • build processes that will outlast any single contributor

The makerspace is a collective effort, and my role has been to help create the conditions for that collective to succeed.
