Esio ApS · Oct 2023 – Present · Denmark

  • SaaS

Problem
Small businesses plan their finances in spreadsheets that drift away from their books.
Solution
A budget app that takes actual figures from the accounting system and works out VAT and tax, built and operated as technical co-founder.

Overview:

Technical co-founder of Esio ApS, responsible for the architecture, development and operation of a multi-tenant financial SaaS platform for small businesses and one-person companies. The full story of the platform is in the SaaS Platform for Financial Workflows case study.

Key contributions:

  • Architecting and developing the full SaaS platform on a DDD-based .NET backend with EF Core and PostgreSQL.
  • Building Docker-based infrastructure with Traefik, automated certificates and secure configuration.
  • Implementing CI/CD with GitHub Actions for container publishing, provisioning and production deployment.
  • Designing business workflows, schemas and data pipelines with a focus on correctness and maintainability.
  • Managing the full product lifecycle: requirements, UX design, customer value mapping and the long-term roadmap.
  • Deploying Prometheus and Grafana monitoring for lightweight metrics collection.
