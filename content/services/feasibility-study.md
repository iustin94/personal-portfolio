---
title: "Feasibility study"
weight: 2
description: "A measured answer to whether a technology or a design will hold, before you commit money to it."
situation: "You are about to commit to a technology or a design, and nobody has measured whether it will hold."
deliverables:
  - "A baseline and a test plan agreed before testing starts"
  - "Reproducible tests against your own workload"
  - "A written report: what holds, what breaks and at what load"
  - "A recommendation: go, change the design, or stop"
