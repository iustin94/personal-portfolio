**Overview:**

Developed a software platform for the Cognition and Behavior Laboratory at Aarhus University to investigate biometric synchronization during shared cognitive and vocal tasks.

**Key contributions:**

- Designed and developed a software platform to investigate biometric synchronization during shared cognitive and vocal tasks.
- Engineered real-time data synchronization across heterogeneous input streams, including heart rate sensors, Wii remotes, and audio recordings.
- Implemented custom Wii remote integration, translating accelerometer data into precise on-screen cursor control.
- Built a concurrent threading architecture to ensure low-latency, high-throughput data capture and alignment across all devices.
- Integrated audio recording pipelines with minimal latency, enabling reliable multimodal data collection in a complex experimental environment.
- Delivered an innovative technical foundation for future research despite project cancellation due to budget constraints.
